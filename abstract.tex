
\begin{abstract}
Rubin is now about one year in operations and Data Management (DM) has delivered several Data Previews including DP2 which contains good commissioning data.
We will share some results of our pipelines, discuss some of the challenges of moving and processing terabytes of data from Chile and to serving thousands of users.
We will mention a few lessons learned.
Rubin has also transitioned from a stand-alone project to being embedded in an organization - we shall ponder organizational inertia and team vs organizational clashes.
\end{abstract}
